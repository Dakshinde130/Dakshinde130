## &nbsp;`02` &nbsp;·&nbsp; The Work

<br/>

> *Four projects. Each one a question I needed to answer.*

<br/>

### &nbsp;&nbsp;◆ &nbsp;[Anikator](https://github.com/Dakshinde130/anikator) &nbsp;·&nbsp; *Anime Character Guessing Engine*

> *"Can I build something that reads minds — using only probability?"*
>
> A character-guessing AI that asks the sharpest question, eliminates the most possibilities, and converges on an answer — human intuition, formalized into math. Built solo, end to end.

`Python` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Bayesian Inference` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Decision Trees` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `React`

<br/>

### &nbsp;&nbsp;◆ &nbsp;[SmartStyle AI](https://github.com/Dakshinde130/SmartStyle-AI-based-Family-Wardrobe-and-Outfit-Recommendation-System) &nbsp;·&nbsp; *Wardrobe Intelligence System*

> *"What if your wardrobe knew you better than you knew yourself?"*
>
> Full-stack fashion-intelligence — computer vision reads your clothing, context drives the recommendations. Solo, couple, and family modes. OTP auth. Cloud-synced. Built to deploy, not to demo.

`Python` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Computer Vision` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `React` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Supabase`

<br/>

### &nbsp;&nbsp;◆ &nbsp;[SkillSwap](https://github.com/Dakshinde130/skillswap) &nbsp;·&nbsp; *Peer-to-Peer Knowledge Exchange*

> *"Why does knowledge have a price tag between people who need it?"*
>
> Students teach what they know, learn what they don't. No money, no hierarchy — just direct human exchange. A product built around a conviction, not a feature.

`Python` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Streamlit` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Pandas` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `JSON`

<br/>

### &nbsp;&nbsp;◆ &nbsp;[Recipe Generator](https://github.com/Dakshinde130/recipe-generator) &nbsp;·&nbsp; *Intelligent Culinary Engine*

> *"How do I build something extensible by design, not by accident?"*
>
> Ingests preferences, queries external APIs, returns curated suggestions — on a clean, modular OOP architecture where each piece does one thing well.

`Java` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `REST APIs` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Object-Oriented Design`
